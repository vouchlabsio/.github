Vouch Labs
==========

> Proof that a real, unique human stands behind a piece of content

Anonymous, sybil-resistant, and verified on Stellar with zero-knowledge proofs. Attribution, not authorship — accountability, not AI detection.

### Focus

- Vouch: a verified human stakes an anonymous vouch on content; the Groth16 proof is generated in the browser and the attestation is recorded on Stellar
- Verify: anyone can check how many unique verified humans stand behind a piece of content
- For agents (x402): an HTTP 402 endpoint where an AI agent pays a micropayment and gets an on-chain answer
